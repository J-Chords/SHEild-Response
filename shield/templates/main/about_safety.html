Your safety matters

Privacy choices and safer browsing

Technology cannot guarantee safety. These practical steps can reduce some common risks.

Wellbeing and choice

Safety can look different for every person and family.

Use only the options that feel appropriate for your circumstances. A trusted device, a safer contact method and support from people you choose can all form part of a personal safety plan.

The people pictured represent everyday family wellbeing and are not presented as survivors or service users.

↗

Use Quick Exit

The persistent Quick Exit button replaces this page with a neutral search page. It cannot erase browser history or activity monitored by another person.

◌

Choose a safer device

If possible, use a device and email account that someone else cannot access. Private browsing may still be visible to networks or device monitoring tools.

⌁

Control contact

Only provide a phone number or contact method if it is safe. The report form lets you describe a safer contact preference.

◇

Prototype limits

SHEild Response is a final-year academic project. Submitting a report or SOS here does not notify any real authority or service organisation.
